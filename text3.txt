1. What is the difference between Univariate, Bivariate, and Multivariate analysis?
Answer:-
Univariate Analysis: Analysis of one variable.

Bivariate Analysis: Relationship between two variables.

Multivariate Analysis: Analysis of more than two variables together.

2. How do you interpret a Correlation Heatmap?
Answer:-
A heatmap shows correlation values between variables:
Close to +1 → strong positive relation
Close to -1 → strong negative relation
Close to 0 → weak relation

3. What is the purpose of a Box Plot?
Answer:-
A box plot is used to:
Show data distribution
Detect outliers
Display median and quartiles.

4. How do you identify outliers visually?
Answer:-
Outliers are points far away from the normal distribution in:
Box plots
Scatter plots
Histograms

5. Why is EDA important before modeling?
Answer:-
EDA helps:
Understand data
Find missing values
Detect outliers
Discover patterns
Improve model accuracy
